The roadmap — Louisiana first, then Nebraska

One season, one direction of travel: form the community online, open in Louisiana, deepen it, cross to Nebraska, then certify skills and close with a demo-day finale. Each phase hands the next a concrete asset — people, then projects, then teams, then certified skills. Every stop is planned: cities are named only where the season plan names them, and every venue is TBD until it is signed and confirmed on Luma.

Phase 0 · Nov 19 – Dec 17, 2026

Five Thursday nights · 6:00–7:30pm Central · Online

  • Welcome to the Tour — Session 01 (Dec 4)
  • Community Night (Nov 19)
  • Build Night (Dec 3)
  • Info Night: Sponsors, Training & Incubators (Dec 10)
  • Pre-Season Prep (Dec 17)

The goal: a formed community before anyone travels — SF and Midwest builders meet, first projects ship, and sponsors and venues get one clean briefing night.

  • Lafayette: Cajun Code Sprint (Mar 5–7)

The goal: a proof weekend, then repeat presence instead of a fly-by — founder skills, local-economy build themes (health, port & logistics, energy), and first employer connections.

Phase 2 · Mar 26 – Apr 15, 2027

Nebraska — build & pitch

Omaha & Lincoln are the plan's Nebraska anchor cities · venues TBD

  • Nights & Weekends: Prairie Builder Night (Apr 8)

The goal: agtech and bioeconomy builds, finance and logistics themes — and a pitch night that turns builds into teams with names, decks, and next steps.

Phase 3 · Apr 22 – May 1, 2027

Certify & close the season

Venues TBD · Central

  • BuildFest Skills & Certs Day (Apr 22)
  • Grand Finale (May 1) — season closing

The goal: certifications and a demo-day finale — the cohort that internships, incubator follow-through, and next-season teams are built from.

What success looks like — the plan, not a promise: builders in two states with shipped projects and certified skills; local teams with names, decks, and a next step they chose; universities and employers who met the tour as founding hosts and partners; and a training community that outlasts the season. None of that is confirmed today — it is what the phases above are designed to produce, one written yes at a time.

Sponsor the tour

The tour is seeking sponsors — cash and/or product credits. In return, the tour team works as your brand ambassadors and developer-relations crew on the road: workshops and demos on your tools, your stack in the curriculum, hackathon tracks and judging, tour content, and first access to the builders we train. Every sponsorship number is proposed and negotiable — nothing is promised here, and no sponsor is named until they've signed.

Employer Partner is a sponsor category of its own: employers who join the tour in that category meet the builders the tour trains, in person, on the road. It is a category open to inquiries — no employer is an Employer Partner until they have signed, and none is named here before then.

Organizations interested in hosting, sponsoring, or partnering are welcome to write in — nothing is a partnership until it is signed in writing, and no institution is presented as a partner on this site before then.
