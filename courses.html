The BuilderSpace Academy — course catalogue

Training for builders, taught from work actually done. Formats across the Academy: 90-minute sessions $79 · 3-hour studios $149 · half-day builds $249. Every course below is training — it ends in a working artefact and a record of your finished work.

Enrollment opens when checkout is live. Joining the waitlist costs nothing and holds your place in line — it is not a purchase, and no payment will be taken until the enrollment page says so in plain words.
No promises we can't keep: no course here guarantees a job, a client, an income, or an exam result. What we stand behind is the work itself — real tools, real labs, and limits printed where you can read them before you pay.

Programme: THE AGENT FOUNDRY

First Agent, Production Grade VT-A1

For: first-time agent builders who can already write some code. You leave with: a production-grade agent — tool use, error handling, and a deployment log — built along from Fifteen Agents (B1).

Pair Work: Claude Code in a Real Codebase VT-A2

For: working developers. You leave with: a disciplined pair-work routine — briefs, reviews, and diffs you can defend — practised on a real codebase, not a demo repo.

Proof Before Ship: Evals, Safety & Cost VT-A3

For: anyone shipping an agent other people will use. You leave with: an eval set, a safety pass, and a cost model for your own agent.

The MCP Studio: Ship a Server Other Agents Can Trust VT-A4

For: builders connecting agents to real systems. You leave with: a working MCP server, documented the way another agent — or another team — can rely on.

Programme: REPO OPS

Branch, Review, Ship: Git & GitHub Operations VT-B1

For: working techs and small teams. You leave with: a branch-review-ship routine and a repo that reads like a comic book — vivid teaching panels, preview links, and an agent walkthrough. Bench companion: G4, Repos & Rollbacks.

Pipelines That Fail Loudly: Actions CI/CD & Pages Deploys VT-B2

For: anyone whose deploys fail quietly. You leave with: a CI/CD pipeline on GitHub Actions and a Pages deploy you can roll back at 2 a.m.

Programme: THE DEFENSIBLE SERVER

VPS From Zero VT-C1

For: builders renting their first server. You leave with: a running VPS — Linux, SSH, services — set up in the open and hardened in the session. Bench companion: G5, VPS From Zero (on the Foundation shelf).

Mail You Can Prove: Hostinger Mail & DNS Operations VT-C2

For: operators who send real email. You leave with: mail and DNS records you can verify byte-for-byte, and the habit of proving delivery instead of assuming it.

Programme: EDGE SCHOOL

Zones Without Fear: DNS & Records Done Right VT-D1

For: anyone afraid of breaking DNS — which is everyone, once. You leave with: a zone you understand record by record, and a change routine that never breaks mail.

Edge Publishing: Workers, Pages & the x402 Pattern VT-D2

For: builders publishing at the edge. You leave with: a Worker and a Pages site live, plus the x402 pay-per-call pattern worked end to end.

Programme: THE INBOX STANDARD

Mail That Lands: Domains, Authentication & First Sends on Resend VT-E1

For: teams setting up transactional email. You leave with: an authenticated sending domain (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) and a first send you verified in the recipient's inbox.

Deliverability Operations: Warm-Up, Lanes & Suppression Law VT-E2

For: anyone sending at volume. You leave with: a warm-up schedule, sending lanes, and a suppression discipline that keeps dead addresses dead. Bench companion: G8, Landed.

Programme: THE RENDER QUEUE

Prompt to Finished Cut: The Reactor Workflow VT-F1

For: builders making AI film. You leave with: a finished cut, produced prompt-to-export, with the download law observed — a film counts only when a verified file is on disk.

Slates & Series: Video Production at Scale VT-F2

For: producers running more than one film at a time. You leave with: a slate plan — blocks, budgets, and a distribution pipeline — the way the network runs its own series.

Programme: THE PROMPT LIBRARY

Prompt Engineering for Working Teams VT-I1

For: team leads. You leave with: a shared prompt library your team actually uses — versioned, reviewed, and written for the work, not the demo.

Scoped & Delivered: AI Consulting Engagements VT-I2

For: practitioners taking on client work. You leave with: a scoped engagement document — boundaries, deliverables, and delivery discipline — ready to adapt to your next client.
