How this becomes one click

The manual process behind these charts is documented step-by-step in the Agent Training Guide (AGENT_TRAINING_GUIDE.md in the one-click playbook), including the 16 holdup patterns mined from this sprint and the mapping from every manual step to the One-Apply system features (F1–F11) that automate it: gates before drafting, a capture-point tracker for expiring codes, a human-step stager that batches the founder's taps into one sitting, duplicate and one-applicant guards, a verified-figures service so numbers never drift, and receipt capture so "submitted" is always earned. The goal: a founder picks a programme, and the system does everything except the steps only a human may do.
