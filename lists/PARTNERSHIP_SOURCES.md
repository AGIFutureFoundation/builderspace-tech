## Honesty & Staleness Flags

- **Railway Startup Program (from our own prior check):** announced Apr 2024 ($5K, $2M–$25M raised required); application paths 404 as of Nov 2026 — a dated announcement, not a live programme. Pattern to watch for everywhere: a changelog/blog announcement is not an open programme.
- **Superteam Instagrants:** listings churn; at least one long-running example page is still live (6.7), but assume any specific grant may be paused until Earn shows it open.
- **awesome-list figures (2.1, 2.2):** amounts are advertised maximums captured months ago; several programmes in these lists have since changed tiers or paused (e.g., directory 2.3 notes DigitalOcean Hatch "paused until early 2027"). Always re-verify on the provider page.
- **Aggregator totals (Cat 1):** platform headline numbers are sums of all partner offers' maximums, quoted here only as the platforms' own claims.
- **SponsorMyEvent (5.1):** free to list, but the 15% commission on closed deals is real money on success — factor it into package pricing.
- **MLH Member Events (3.1):** the ≥4-months-out application rule means early tour stops (Nov–Dec 2026) cannot become member events retroactively; the route fits the 2027 stops.
