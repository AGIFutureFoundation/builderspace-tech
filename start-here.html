Start here

BuilderSpace brings the coding and startup world from San Francisco and Silicon Valley to the towns on the tour — to empower local builders, match them with potential employers, and bridge the tech divide. Here is the way in, in three steps.

1Read

Start with the tour guide — what BuilderSpace is, how a session runs, and how towns, schools, and employers take part. Then read the founder-credits guide: the credit and programme offers worth filing for, in the order that keeps every application alive.

2Learn

Pick one course from the Academy catalogue and join its waitlist — agent engineering if you build software, Repo Ops if you run a team, Edge School if DNS has ever beaten you. The companion books are Fifteen Agents and Second Cities, First Principles, catalogued in Resources.

3Build

Come to Session 01 — Friday, December 4, 2026, 6:00–7:30 PM Central, online: the tour's opening session, where new builders meet the network, form teams, and leave with a first build underway. Sessions are listed on Luma (the tour calendar is at luma.com — search "BuilderSpace"); write to events@builderspace.tech and a human will route you to the current listing.
