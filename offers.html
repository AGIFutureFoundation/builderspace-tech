What makes a good offer

Concrete value

Say exactly what a builder walks away with — the credit, the tool, the seat, the meal — not a general promise of support.

Clear terms

Name the limits up front: who qualifies, how many there are, what is excluded. Builders trust offers whose fine print is on the front.

A real expiry

Give the offer an end date. A deadline gets it claimed; an open-ended offer gets forgotten.

One link

One link that takes a builder straight to claiming or learning more — not a homepage they have to dig through.
