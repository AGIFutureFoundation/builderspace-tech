BuilderSpace · Founders One-Apply

One application. Every programme you qualify for.

Startup credits, grants and founder programmes are scattered across dozens of providers, each with its own form and its own fine print. Founders One-Apply collects your facts once, matches them against the 418 programmes in our playbook database, runs each programme's published gates, and prepares your applications. The final step is always yours — your account, your attestation, on the provider's own site.

FREE TO APPLY — NO SUCCESS FEES — GATE REASONS NEVER PAYWALLED

How it works

Four steps. The first is the only one that asks anything of you.

Step 1

Apply once

One form: who you are, what you are building, your entity, stage and funding. Every fact is collected exactly once and reused across every programme — no re-typing the same answers into forty different forms.

Step 2

The matcher runs the gates

Your facts are checked against every programme's published eligibility gates in the database. Programmes you do not qualify for come back with the reason stated plainly — never a silent no, and never a closest-match fudge.

Step 3

Packets are prepared

Each match becomes a packet pre-filled from your one form, with the provider's own steps laid out. Anything only you can do — creating the provider account, an ID check, a card step — is spelled out as your step, in advance.

Step 4

You file, we track

Submission happens on the provider's site under your own account and attestation. From there we track each programme's published review clock with you, so you know when an answer is due — and when silence means it is time to follow up.

Proven on our own journey first

This system was built by running it. Before it was offered to anyone else, our own estate — AGI Future Foundation, a San Francisco nonprofit public benefit corporation, bootstrapped, $0 raised — went through it end to end. Every programme in the database has a playbook written from that live run: the required fields, the gotchas, the review clock, and the outcome, recorded as it happened.
