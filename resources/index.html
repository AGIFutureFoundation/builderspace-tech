For employers

Employer Needs — tell us what you need from new hires

The local-economy intake: the roles you are hiring, the skills a new hire must walk in with, the tools they should already know. Your answers become the training targets for the tour towns.

Win — win — win

For vendors: we act as DevRel — we learn a tool the way its makers hope someone will, teach it in the open, and put it in builders' hands with a first project attached, so a trial becomes a workload instead of a forgotten tab.
For local companies: we ask what you need from new hires and train toward it — a credit spent training toward a real job description is a credit that hired somebody. The intake is the Employer Needs page.
Through universities: the channel we are building — the tools and the training reach students where they already are, in the towns the tour serves. No university is named as a partner until it has agreed in writing; until then, this is the direction of travel, stated plainly.
