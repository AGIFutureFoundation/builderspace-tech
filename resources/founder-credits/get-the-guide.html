What it teaches: the credits system in four moves

Credits are not a lottery; they are a system with an order of operations. Most founders lose programmes not because they were ineligible, but because they claimed in the wrong order, believed a headline number, or let a balance expire. The guide teaches the four moves that prevent all three.

Move 1

Classify every programme

Sort the landscape before you touch a form: which programmes are self-serve, which are partner- or referral-gated, which are worth filing now, and which to prepare for later. The gated headline tiers are real, but they are not the tier a pre-seed founder claims first.

Move 2

Verify in the console

A programme counts when the balance shows in the provider's own console — not when an email, a headline, or a prize announcement says so. The guide shows how to check what you actually hold before you plan spend around it.

Move 3

Sequence before signup

Several programmes disqualify you for claiming them in the wrong order — creating an account first can void a starter offer, and three master keys (a live website, a domain-matched email, the right entity) have to exist before the applications that screen for them.

Move 4

Track everything

Credits are perishable. Expiry dates, good-standing hygiene, and a tracking sheet somebody actually maintains are the difference between a stack that funds a year of building and a stack that evaporates.

Who it is for

Early-stage founders — pre-seed, bootstrapped, or just incorporated — who keep hearing that there is a fortune in free credits out there and want to know what is actually claimable, in what order, and what quietly disqualifies you. It is also for founders building through a nonprofit: that is a completely separate track with its own verification gatekeepers, and the guide gives it its own chapter rather than mixing the two tracks — mixing them is the fastest way to get rejected from both.
