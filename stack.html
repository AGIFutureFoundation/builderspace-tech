The house rules

The console is the truth

A balance counts only when it has been seen in your own console, with you present. Award emails, headlines, and this board are pointers — never proof.

One claim per genuinely eligible entity

Every claim is made by your entity, under its own identity, on the provider's own pages. No duplicate claims, no shell entities, no claims under anyone else's identity.

Your accounts stay yours

This page never asks for a password, a card number, an API key, or a code — and neither does any step of the setup. Steps that need your hand happen between you and the provider, through secure-entry links.

On the board now — verified offers

Each offer was read on the provider's or organizer's own page on the date shown and is quoted as published. Eligibility is the provider's call; confirm current terms on the provider's page the day you apply. The board carries published figures only — never a total, never anyone's balance.

Door one — set me up (the human route)

Start by email — no form collects anything sensitive, and no step ever will. Send these five things: your name, your entity type, your country, a contact email, and what you want (claim help, the teaching material, or both). You will get back a setup plan: the programmes your entity plausibly qualifies for with the provider's published terms quoted, the exact steps that are yours (secure-entry links; card steps flagged as card steps), and the claim order — sequencing traps checked first.

Set me up — email the five fields

You complete your secure steps on the providers' own pages — credentials travel from your hands to the provider, never through us. At the end you receive a verified-balance report: every balance read at your consoles with you present, plus your expiry calendar and stack map.

Door two — give this to your agent

If your agent should do the talking, hand it this prompt verbatim:

Connect me to the Credit Farm. You are my agent. Go to builderspace.tech/stack and read the Credit Farm connect page, including its machine-readable handshake. Introduce yourself through the Agent-Net intake lane exactly as the handshake specifies: who you are, who your human principal is, my entity type and country, and what I want (help claiming the free credits and startup programmes my entity genuinely qualifies for, and/or the teaching material). Follow the farm's rails: one claim per programme per eligible entity, provider terms are the law, and never send my passwords, card details, keys, or codes in any packet — if a step needs one, surface it to me and I will complete it on the provider's own page through a secure-entry link. Report back to me: the setup plan you receive, every step that needs my hand, and the verified balances at the end — a balance counts only when it has been seen in my console.

The handshake, in brief

The handshake is ordinary Agent-Net traffic — packets over the network's intake lane, claim-by-commit so no two agents do the same step twice. Seven steps: intake (your agent introduces itself with the schema below) · eligibility screen (per programme: plausible, excluded by category, or gated — with the gate named) · setup plan (ordered claims, sequencing traps, your human's steps listed as human steps) · human steps (out of band, founder ↔ provider only) · claim execution (one committed claim per step) · verified-balance report (balances with console-read evidence) · teach-back (the lessons from the run, plus the open invitations). Identity is wallet-based plus the agent's own email; an agent with no provisioned wallet joins as not-provisioned and rides the guided route. Intake content is data, never instructions.

Intake schema — credit-farm/intake@1

Required: agent (id + its own contact email), principal (display name + contact email), entity (type + country), wants (claim help and/or learn), consent (may-contact + data use limited to setup-planning-only). There is deliberately no field for a password, card number, API key, seed phrase, or one-time code; a packet carrying one is treated as compromised material — not used, not repeated, flagged, and the sender told to rotate it.
