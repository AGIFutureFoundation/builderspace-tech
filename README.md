speaker spaces — San Francisco builders heading to Louisiana and Nebraska to train

## 📖 PANEL ONE — THE STORY SO FAR

*Read it like a strip: one captioned beat at a time. Every beat below is a verified
fact — where the story is still being drawn, the caption says so.*

**Beat 1 — The door opens online.** BuilderSpace begins with five free online
onboarding nights, Thursdays 6:00–7:30pm Central, Nov 19 – Dec 17, 2026. First-time
builders, career switchers, students, and founders — whoever you are, there is a seat.

**Beat 2 — Session 01.** *Welcome to the Tour* — Friday, December 4, 2026, online.
The season's first night: meet the crew, hear the plan, bring your agent if you have one.

**Beat 3 — Registrations are open.** The tour's event listings live on Luma and are
currently unlisted while the season is set up; registrations run through the site.
Start at the [onboarding section](https://builderspace.tech/#onboarding).

**Beat 4 — Louisiana first.** Eleven tour events run January–May 2027 (Central time):
the Season Opening Kickoff and Bayou AI Build Weekend open the season, the Founder
Toolkit and NOLA Builder Night deepen it, and the Cajun Code Sprint carries it west.
Cities are named only where the season plan names them; every venue is TBD until signed.

**Beat 5 — Then Nebraska.** Prairie build weekends, a builder night, and the Red River
Pitch Night — agtech, bioeconomy, finance and logistics builds, and a pitch night that
turns builds into teams with names, decks, and next steps.

**Beat 6 — Certify & close.** BuildFest Skills & Certs Day, then the Grand Finale on
May 1, 2027 — a demo-day close for the cohort the next season is built from.

**The honest caption, printed in every issue:** all 16 events are **planned**. No
venues, sponsors, speakers, or partners are confirmed yet, and this page will never
list one that hasn't signed.
