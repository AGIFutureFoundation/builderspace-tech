Founders One-Apply · a BuilderSpace programme

One application. Every programme you actually qualify for.

Tell us who you are — once. The matcher checks every programme in the database against every gate that programme publishes — entity type, funding, country, determination-letter, incorporation, referral, sector — and gives you a reason for every single result. No silent passes, no silent failures.

What one click honestly means: one application with us produces a truthful match report and a copy-ready packet for every match. Submission happens on the provider's site, under your own account and attestation. Anything only you can do — creating a provider account, an ID check, entering a card — is spelled out as your step, in advance, per programme.

New in v3 — the One-Click Apply Pack: the full review now ends with a Send Queue that sorts every match into three honestly labelled lanes — EMAIL-ROUTE (the application can be sent as one email; the packet text is ready for you to send from your own mailbox), FOUNDER-PORTAL (the programme requires your own account/portal; the packet, the portal address, and the exact account needed are listed), and NEEDS-INPUT (the packet is ready and one named input is still missing — the input is named, per programme). The whole pack downloads as one file. One-Apply does not auto-submit anything, to anyone, ever — email-route packets are ready for you to send, and portal applications are filed by you under your own account and attestation.
